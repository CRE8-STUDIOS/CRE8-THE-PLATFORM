## [1.0.0] (2026-11-24)

### Added

- SvelteKit 3 project with Supabase (database, auth and storage)
- Login and signup for freelancers and clients, with email confirmation
- Route protection by role (freelancer or client) in `hooks.server.js`
- Atomic design components: atoms, molecules and organisms
- Overview of the day and welcome screen with clock-in
- Clock-in: per project, one clock at a time, "Still working?" check and automatic stop when CRE8 is closed
- Clients and projects, with contracts, requests (extra work), files, updates and portal invites
- Agenda: week view, inbox, meetings, shoots, errands, rest, checklists and people
- Time overview with the 1,225-hour criterion
- Facturen with YYMMNN numbers, lines, sending, payments and credit facturen
- Expenses with receipts and btw guidance per quarter
- Moodboards and notes
- Settings: business details, clock and meetings, working hours, theme, app buttons, contract templates and tax rules
- Client portal: projects, signing contracts and requests, asking for extra work, booking meetings, facturen, files and moodboards
